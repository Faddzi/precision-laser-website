About Us

At Precision Laser Cutting Solutions (PLCS), we specialize in metal fabrication, delivering high-precision results for industries, designers, and individual clients across Mumbai, Navi Mumbai and Thane. With years of hands-on experience and a deep understanding of fabrication techniques, we’ve built a reputation for quality craftsmanship, reliable turnaround times, and cost-effective solutions.

Whether it’s a custom prototype or a full-scale production run, our expert team uses advanced equipment to provide services that include metal laser cutting, metal bending, press brake bending, and complete fabrication assemblies. We work only with metals, ensuring complete focus and expertise in handling a wide variety of grades and thicknesses. From stainless steel and aluminum to mild steel and more.

Our Mission

To turn your designs into durable, precision-engineered metal products quickly, accurately, and affordably.

We cater to:
- Industrial manufacturers in need of volume-based production
- Architects and designers seeking custom components
- Engineers and fabricators looking for precision parts
- Individual clients with one-off or small-batch requirements

Every project, no matter the scale, is handled with attention to detail and a commitment to quality. When you work with PLCS, you get more than just a service — you get a fabrication partner.
